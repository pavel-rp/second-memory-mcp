**What is tracked:** one row per **material claim** made in `../00_method-and-provenance.md` and `../01_provenance-and-rights.md`, each carrying its **Class**, **Evidence type**, **Cutoff**, **Provenance** (file · section) and **Structural limitation**. Ids are namespaced **`RG-`** (rights ground) and the completeness checks **`ROC-`** (`-R`), so neither collides with NEU-899's `OC-*`, NEU-932's `SOC-*`/`F-*`, or any sibling sub-task's ids. **No new taxonomy, class, or relation type is defined here.**

**The one thing to read before the table.** Every class-1 row in this register was produced by a **documentary re-read of a recorded prior reading**, not by contacting a source: **no network access was available or permitted to this sub-task, and zero HTTP requests were issued** (`../00_method-and-provenance.md` §4.1). The *Evidence type* column says so on every affected row, and the *Structural limitation* column says what that costs. A reader who skips this paragraph must still not be able to mistake a re-read for a fetch — that is why the limitation is repeated per row rather than hoisted into a footnote.
