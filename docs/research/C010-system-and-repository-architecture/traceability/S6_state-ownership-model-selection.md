`OUT-3`'s "Verified by" clause has four parts. **Three are discharged above** — the criteria
set with weights and sources fixed before scoring, the side-by-side scored comparison with
per-score evidence, and the selected-and-rejected model records with migration paths and
residual uncertainty.

state-inventory↔matrix audit — is not**, and its row says so rather than being omitted. That
work is `SUB-13 (NEU-977)`'s, audited by `SUB-14 (NEU-978)`, and this chapter's contribution to
it is the assignment rule of `../07_…md` §6 plus the two demonstrations in §6.2 — a termination
demonstration over two rows, not a census over 45. An outcome with a partially-covered
verification clause is `NEU-986 (SUB-12)`'s to weigh at the completeness gate; presenting
`OUT-3` as fully verified by this sub-task alone would have been false.

`OUT-10` is discharged in full for **this sub-task's own claims**. It is a package-level
outcome — every sub-task that leans on an uncertain and material claim owes the same
discipline — so the rows above cover SUB-6's two claims and no one else's.
