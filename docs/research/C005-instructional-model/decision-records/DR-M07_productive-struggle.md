# DR-M07 — Productive Struggle

- **Record id / mechanism:** DR-M07 · Productive struggle · cluster §C-FBK · author NEU-920 · 2026-07-13
- **Learning-critical:** no  (source: template §3 — struggle mis-calibration wastes germane load and is coupled to M06, but M07 does not itself produce or gate the graded mastery signal)

## Decision (observable behavior)

The system **deliberately withholds the canonical answer for a bounded attempt sequence** — a real first attempt, then one **diagnostic-hint-scaffolded retry** — before revealing the answer, keeping the task inside the accomplishable band via retrievability-tiered scaffolding: open-ended recall is preserved while the item is within band, and support degrades to recognition-level **only when the retrievability signal falls below the low bar** (item outside the band). A reviewer or a test can watch that (a) on the first failure the system emits a diagnostic-pivot hint and **re-prompts open-ended** rather than immediately revealing the answer, (b) the reveal happens at the terminal attempt (handing off to M06's correct-answer exposure), and (c) degradation to recognition occurs iff the retrievability signal is below the bar. Struggle is bounded, resolved, and never open-ended.

This is **observable/testable** (a hint-then-open-reprompt is emitted; degradation is gated on a signal), not an intention to "make the learner struggle."

## Cited evidence + class

- F-M07-1 [class 1, causal] — Effortful, errorful attempts enhance learning relative to error-avoidant study — *provided* there is subsequent correct-answer exposure (M06). Supports the *causal* claim that a real attempt before help beats immediate reveal.
- F-M07-2 [class 1, principle over causal base] — Struggle is productive only within the accomplishable band ("the task must be able to be accomplished"). Bounds the withholding.
- F-M07-3 [class 1, Metcalfe causal / Challenge Point analogical] — The productive zone is learner-state-dependent (region of proximal learning; inverted-U): gain is maximal for items close to but not yet mastered, low when difficulty is too low or too high. Supports the state-dependent tiering.
- F-M07-4 [class 1, causal (structure) + empirical caveat] — A real second attempt with a diagnostic hint before reveal is supported, but its value depends on the hint correctly diagnosing the failure — where AI tutors are weak (F-M06-4); no study pins the optimal number of attempts ("2" is not evidence-derived, gap G6).
- F-M07-5 [class 2, code-evidence] — The existing system operationalizes a 2-attempt-with-pivot-hint structure and retrievability-tiered scaffolding (recognition only at R<0.3), broadly tracking the accomplishable band but as a coarse binary flip. **Compatibility fact only** — availability, not pedagogical endorsement.

Causal behavioral claim (attempt-before-help beats immediate reveal) rests on F-M07-1/3. The hint-quality caveat rests on F-M07-4 / F-M06-4. No class-2 finding is used as endorsement.

## Constraints

- **Cognitive-load / desirable-difficulty:** Productive struggle **is** the desirable-difficulty dial — it *is* the mechanism that spends **germane load**. It manages **intrinsic** load by capping struggle within the accomplishable band (recognition tier for high-intrinsic-load DP derivations the learner cannot yet hold in working memory) and removes **extraneous** load via the diagnostic pivot rather than an unstructured re-ask. **Desirable difficulty — preserved vs scaffolded (this record's canonical statement):** struggle is deliberately **PRESERVED** during the first attempt and the hinted open-ended retry (this is the canonical desirable difficulty for this feature); it is deliberately **SCAFFOLDED/REMOVED** in exactly two places — (i) when the retrievability signal drops below the low bar the tier degrades to recognition to keep the task accomplishable (F-M07-2/3), and (ii) at the terminal attempt where the answer is revealed so struggle resolves into correction (hand-off to M06). Too little struggle wastes the germane-load opportunity; too much exceeds working memory and becomes unproductive.
- **Durable-vs-speed (framework `../framework/00_…`) — material, staged resolution (A):** The hint-depth / attempt-budget dial has **opposing gradients** — more struggle (deeper withholding) builds durable schema (F-M07-1 causal; F-DD-2) but *depresses* immediate throughput; less struggle (faster reveal / shallower band) protects immediate fluency but forfeits the largest gain (F-DD-2). **T1 PASS** (opposing gradient), **T2 PASS-asymmetric** (durable side causal F-M07-1; speed/immediate-cost side directional F-DD-2), **T3 PASS** (both goals in-objective for the CP audience). → **Material → staged (A):** struggle is preserved in the **acquisition/learning phase**; the attempt budget is compressed and hint latency reduced in a later **contest-speed/fluency phase**, gated by an observable **per-item fluency signal** (accuracy/latency crossing a bar; **value UNRESOLVED → LINK-I2**, shape fixed now). Dual-goal evidence cited (durable F-M07-1; speed F-DD-2); no silent single-goal optimization.

## Rejected alternative

- **Error-avoidant study / immediate reveal (no struggle)** — rejected because F-M07-1 and F-DD-2 show forfeiting the low-retrieval-strength attempt forfeits the largest learning gain; the reveal-first design optimizes immediate comfort at the cost of durable schema.
- **Unbounded struggle / no reveal** — rejected because F-M07-2 shows too-difficult a task dissuades the learner and prevents full processing, and F-M07-1 does not endorse struggle without resolution. This is why the withholding is *bounded* and always resolves.
